Overview

What it does

Given requirement documents, test-case sheets, and a defect list, the Requirements Traceability Matrix Generator skill builds a bidirectional RTM — mapping business and functional requirements to test cases, execution results, and defects — for coverage proof and audit sign-off. It is the capability that closes the loop, consuming requirements from the BRD / FRD Analyzer and the specs the QA Automation Agent produces.

Its guiding principle: every requirement needs a test-case ID — distinguish "tested" from "passed", and never go live with critical requirements untested. The matrix traces forward from requirement to test and backward from test to requirement, flagging gaps and orphan tests along the way.

Quality

Evaluation criteria

The bar a matrix must clear before it is trusted.

✓  Every requirement assigned at least one test-case ID, or flagged as a gap.
✓  Forward and backward traceability both verified, not just one direction.
✓  "Tested" and "passed" reported as distinct states.
✓  Critical requirements never marked GO while untested.
✓  Orphan tests surfaced, not silently dropped.
